## Why

Players get stuck not knowing what to do next. The hint exists, but only when you press for it, and the room map at the top shows only where you are, not where you need to go.

## What

At the right moments, the game offers Lyra's thought about what to do next without being asked, and the map highlights the room it points to.

- Lyra's next-step thought appears on its own when a puzzle is solved (it says what that unlocked and where), when the player first enters a room with something to do, and when the player has made no progress for a while (about a minute).
- It shows briefly as a thought bubble, doesn't pause the game, and doesn't repeat itself constantly; the Hint button still shows it on demand.
- The room map highlights the room the current hint points to (e.g. pulsing), distinct from the room Lyra is in, and the highlight moves on once that step is done.
