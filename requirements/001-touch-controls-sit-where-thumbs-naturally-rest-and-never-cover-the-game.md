## Why

On a phone in landscape the touch controls are awkward. The direction buttons are stacked oddly (left above right, down beside left), so moving left and right means moving your thumb up and down. The SWAT button overlaps the playfield and the right-side buttons crowd the room. Alex finds the game hard to play on his phone.

## What

On a phone, the touch controls feel like a normal handheld game.

- Left thumb: left and right buttons side by side on one row in the bottom-left corner, with down below or between them, so moving never needs an up-and-down thumb movement.
- Right thumb: JUMP and SWAT in the bottom-right corner, JUMP the larger and nearest the corner, SWAT just above or to its left.
- No button overlaps the room being played; the room is scaled or letterboxed to leave space for them.
- Buttons are large enough to hit without looking (at least 56 CSS px) and stay in place across rooms, in portrait and landscape, and on small and large phones.
- Desktop with a keyboard is unchanged.
