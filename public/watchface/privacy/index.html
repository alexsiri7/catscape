Catscape watch face: privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The Catscape watch face (package net.interstellarai.catscape) is a Wear OS watch face that shows Lyra, the cat from Catscape, with the time and date.

What it collects

Nothing. The watch face does not collect, store, share or send any personal or device data.

  • It contains no code: it is a Watch Face Format resource package that the watch's own system renders.
  • It requests no permissions.
  • It has no network access, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • The time and date it shows come from the watch and never leave it.
  • Tapping the face plays an animation on the watch. Taps are not recorded.

Google Play

If you install the watch face from Google Play, Google handles the download under its own privacy policy. We receive only the aggregate, anonymous statistics that Google Play shows every developer, such as install counts.

Changes

If the watch face ever starts handling data, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact details on the watch face's Google Play listing.
